1 Personal Motivation & Philosophy
Q1 What motivated you to leave a secure, well-paid principal position to start PCFTW? ▼
Why they ask: To test your conviction and authenticity.
English

My decision was driven by a profound sense of moral obligation. For seventeen years, I served in Hong Kong's education system, first as a teacher and eventually as a founding principal. During those years, I witnessed a persistent and troubling pattern: students from low-income families who could not keep up with the curriculum were systematically neglected, left behind, and often discarded. They attended classes but could not follow the lessons. When they fell behind, they were retained. When they misbehaved, they were expelled. The system offered them a seat in the classroom but denied them genuine support to learn and grow. The final catalyst came from the story of a Japanese teacher who spent fourteen years walking the streets at night, accompanying troubled youth. His example gave me the clarity and courage I needed. In 2009, I resigned. I started by going to a sports centre late at night, setting up a table with a banner, and asking troubled youth if they wanted free tutoring. I began with five students. What motivated me was not ambition or recognition. It was the simple, urgent belief that every child deserves the right to learn and grow.

中文

我的決定源於一種深刻的道德責任感。我在香港教育界服務了十七年，從教師做起，最終成為創校校長。那些年裡，我目睹了一個持續而令人不安的現象：來自低收入家庭的學生，因為跟不上課程進度，被系統性地忽視、遺棄，甚至被淘汰。他們坐在教室裡，卻聽不懂課；落後了，就被留級；行為出錯了，就被開除。制度給了他們一個座位，卻沒有給他們真正學習和成長的支援。最終的催化劑來自一位日本教師的故事——他花了十四年時間，每晚在街頭陪伴那些迷途的青年。他的榜樣給了我所需的清晰和勇氣。2009年，我辭職了。我開始在深夜去體育館，擺一張桌子，掛上橫幅，問那些迷途的青年是否想要免費補習。我從五個學生開始。激勵我的不是野心或認可，而是一個簡單而迫切的信念：每個孩子都應該有學習和成長的權利。

Q2 What is the core philosophy behind PCFTW? ▼
Why they ask: To understand the ideological foundation of your work.
English

The core philosophy is "equality of learning rights." This is different from mere equality of access. Formal equality of access—the guarantee that every child can enrol in school and receive twelve years of free education—represents only a surface-level commitment. Genuine equality of learning rights requires more: students must not merely occupy classroom seats but must receive the support necessary for actual intellectual and personal development. We believe that true educational justice means valuing progress over rank, effort over innate ability, and dignity over comparison. This is why we created the "compare only with yourself" scholarship—awarded to any student who improves by even a single point. We also believe that education should be a "third education system" alongside formal schooling and commercial tutoring—a free tutoring system for those who cannot access the first two effectively. And ultimately, we hope to become unnecessary. If Hong Kong's schools genuinely provided appropriate support to all students regardless of family background, the third education system of free tutoring would have no reason to exist.

中文

核心理念是「學習權利的平等」。這不同於單純的入學機會平等。形式上的入學機會平等——保證每個孩子都能入學並接受十二年免費教育——只是表面層次的承諾。真正的學習權利平等需要更多：學生不僅要佔據教室的座位，還必須獲得實際智力和個人發展所需的支援。我們相信，真正的教育公義意味著重視進步多於排名，重視努力多於天賦，重視尊嚴多於比較。這就是為什麼我們創立了「只與自己比較」的獎學金——任何進步哪怕只有一分的學生都能獲獎。我們也相信，教育應該是與正規學校教育和商業補習並列的「第三個教育系統」——為那些無法有效獲得前兩個系統支援的人提供免費補習。而最終，我們希望變得不再被需要。如果香港的學校真正能為所有學生提供適切的支援，不論家庭背景，那麼免費補習的第三個教育系統就沒有存在的理由了。

Q3 Why one-on-one? Why not group tutoring to serve more students? ▼
Why they ask: To test your pedagogical reasoning and commitment to quality.
English

One-on-one tutoring is not the most efficient model on paper. It does not allow us to serve as many students at once as a group class would. But efficiency is not the goal. The goal is genuine, transformative learning for students whom the system has already failed. There are three reasons for this commitment. First, our students arrive with fragile self-esteem and defensive pride. They have been compared unfavourably to others throughout their school lives. In a group setting, they will not ask questions for fear of ridicule. In a one-on-one setting, they can safely acknowledge confusion to a single tutor. Second, genuine differentiated instruction is impossible in larger groups. I learned this the hard way with my first two students. When one progressed faster than the other, the slower student felt he was holding his friend back and withdrew altogether. Third, academic difficulties rarely exist in isolation. Our students face family instability, financial stress, and limited parental support. They will only share these struggles within a relationship of genuine trust, which the one-on-one format, sustained over at least three months, makes possible. For these students—fragile in confidence, distrustful of adults, and carrying years of academic failure—the one-on-one relationship is the only environment in which real learning can take place.

中文

一對一補習在紙面上不是最有效率的模式。它不能像小組班那樣同時服務那麼多學生。但效率不是目標。目標是為那些已被制度辜負的學生提供真正的、轉化性的學習。這個堅持有三個原因。第一，我們的學生帶著脆弱的自尊和防禦性的驕傲來到我們面前。他們在整個校園生活中都被拿來與他人比較。在小組環境中，他們害怕被嘲笑而不敢提問。在一對一的環境中，他們可以安全地向一位導師承認自己的困惑。第二，在較大的群體中，真正的因材施教是不可能的。我是從第一批兩個學生身上學到這個教訓的。當其中一個進步得比另一個快時，較慢的學生覺得自己拖累了朋友，最終退出了補習。第三，學業困難很少是孤立存在的。我們的學生面對家庭不穩定、經濟壓力和有限的父母支援。他們只會在真正信任的關係中分享這些掙扎，而一對一的模式，持續至少三個月，使這種關係成為可能。對於這些信心脆弱、不信任成年人、背負多年學業失敗的學生來說，一對一的關係是唯一能讓真正學習發生的環境。

Q4 What keeps you going after 15+ years? ▼
Why they ask: To see if your passion is genuine and sustainable.
English

Every time I receive a letter from a student saying they got into university, or that they finally understand a subject, or that they want to become a tutor themselves—that is what keeps me going. The "Pay It Forward" effect—where 18.6% of our students become volunteer tutors—tells me this work is not just about me. It is a movement that will outlast me. When I see a student who once felt ashamed of receiving help become a volunteer tutor themselves, I know the work is sustainable. It is not dependent on me. It is a culture that has taken root.

中文

每次我收到學生的信，說他們考上了大學，或者終於明白了一個科目，或者他們自己想成為導師——這就是支撐我繼續前行的動力。「傳遞善意」效應——我們18.6%的學生成為義務導師——告訴我這項工作不僅僅是關於我。這是一場將比我更持久的運動。當我看到一個曾經為接受幫助而感到羞恥的學生成為義務導師時，我知道這項工作是可持續的。它不依賴於我。這是一種已經扎根的文化。

Q5 Who has inspired you most in your life? ▼
Why they ask: To understand your values and sources of motivation.
English

I have been inspired by many people, but the greatest inspiration has come from two sources. First, my mother. She was a teacher, and I saw how her students—decades after graduating—would return to visit her, to thank her, to share their lives with her. I saw the profound, lasting impact a teacher can have. That is when I first thought, "Perhaps I could be a teacher like that." Second, my wife. She was my partner, my supporter, and my greatest source of strength. When I told her I wanted to resign, she did not try to stop me. She simply took out a piece of paper, drew a line down the middle, and began calculating our income and expenses. She said that as long as basic needs were met, she did not object. She bore the economic and psychological pressures of my decision with grace and courage. She passed away two years ago, and I still miss her every day. She taught me what it means to support someone unconditionally. And finally, I am inspired by our students. Their resilience, their courage, their willingness to try again despite years of failure—they inspire me to keep going. They are the reason I do this work. They are the reason I cannot stop.

中文

我受到許多人的啟發，但最大的啟發來自兩個來源。第一，我的母親。她是一位教師，我看到她的學生——畢業幾十年後——會回來探望她、感謝她、與她分享他們的生活。我看到一位教師可以產生的深遠、持久的影響。那是我第一次想：「也許我可以成為那樣的老師。」第二，我的妻子。她是我的伴侶、我的支持者、我最大的力量來源。當我告訴她我想辭職時，她沒有試圖阻止我。她只是拿出一張紙，畫了一條線，開始計算我們的收入和支出。她說只要基本需求得到滿足，她就不反對。她以優雅和勇氣承受了我決定的經濟和心理壓力。她兩年前去世了，我仍然每天想念她。她教會了我無條件支持一個人的意義。最後，我受到我們的學生的啟發。他們的韌性、他們的勇氣、他們儘管經歷多年失敗仍願意再嘗試——他們激勵我繼續前行。他們是我做這項工作的原因。他們是我不能停下來的原因。

2 Program Model & Impact
Q6 How is PCFTW different from other tutoring charities? ▼
Why they ask: To test your model's distinctiveness and scalability.
English

Three things make us different. First, we are truly free—no fees, no means-testing, no stigma. Second, we are one-on-one, not group tutoring, which allows us to address each student's specific needs. Third, we don't just teach academics—we mentor, we build confidence, and we measure outcomes. 62.1% of our students report academic gains, 51.7% report confidence transformation, and 47.1% overcome economic barriers. We are not just a tutoring service; we are a parallel support system.

中文

有三件事使我們與眾不同。第一，我們是真正免費的——沒有費用、沒有入息審查、沒有標籤。第二，我們是一對一，不是小組補習，這使我們能夠針對每個學生的具體需求。第三，我們不僅教授學術——我們提供指導、建立自信，並衡量成果。我們62.1%的學生報告學術進步，51.7%報告自信轉變，47.1%克服經濟障礙。我們不僅僅是一個補習服務；我們是一個平行的支援系統。

Q7 How do you measure the impact of your work? ▼
Why they ask: To verify the credibility of your evidence.
English

We measure impact through multiple dimensions. First, academic improvement: an analysis of 656 student testimonials from 2024-2026 shows that 62.1% of students voluntarily reported academic gains. English scores have risen from 33 to 62 marks; mathematics from 21/100 to 59/100; DSE levels from failing to passing. Many students explicitly credit PCFTW with enabling their admission to university programmes including medicine, engineering, and business. Second, emotional and confidence transformation: 51.7% of students reported significant emotional and confidence transformation. They move from believing "I never thought I could" to "I now believe I can." Third, overcoming economic barriers: 47.1% of students explicitly mentioned financial hardship or an inability to pay for tutoring. They describe PCFTW as their "only hope" or "bridge to a better future." Fourth, the "pay it forward" effect: 18.6% of scholarship recipients explicitly committed to becoming volunteer tutors themselves, creating a self-replicating community of givers. This is the strongest evidence of deep, lasting change. Finally, external validation: our work has been recognized in peer-reviewed academic research published in the European Journal of Education, confirming that fee-free one-on-one tutoring enhances the well-being of disadvantaged adolescents.

中文

我們從多個維度衡量影響。第一，學術進步：對2024至2026年656份學生心聲的分析顯示，62.1%的學生自願報告了學術進步。英文分數從33分升至62分；數學從21/100升至59/100；DSE等級從不合格升至合格。許多學生明確表示，PCFTW幫助他們入讀大學課程，包括醫學、工程和商業。第二，情感和自信轉變：51.7%的學生報告了顯著的情感和自信轉變。他們從相信「我從未想過我能做到」轉變為「我現在相信我能做到」。第三，克服經濟障礙：47.1%的學生明確提到經濟困難或無力支付補習費用。他們將PCFTW描述為「唯一的希望」或「通往更美好未來的橋樑」。第四，「傳遞善意」效應：18.6%的獎學金獲得者明確承諾成為義務導師，創造了一個自我複製的給予者社群。這是深刻、持久改變的最強證據。最後，外部驗證：我們的工作已在《歐洲教育研究期刊》的同行評審學術研究中獲得認可，確認免費一對一補習能提升弱勢青少年的福祉。

Q8 How do you recruit and retain over 20,000 volunteer tutors? ▼
Why they ask: To assess your volunteer management and retention strategies.
English

Recruitment is both easy and difficult. It is easy because there are many kind-hearted people in Hong Kong who want to help. It is difficult because we are asking for a sustained commitment—at least three months, one hour per week, at a fixed time and place. This is very different from one-off volunteering. We recruit through multiple channels: university partnerships, corporate partnerships, media coverage, social media, and word of mouth. Many of our tutors are former students who received help and now want to give back. Retention is built on three pillars. First, we provide training and support: orientation sessions, monthly workshops, teaching resources, and a peer community. Second, we emphasize reciprocity: tutoring benefits the tutor as much as the student. Many tutors report that the experience provides meaningful connection, perspective on social inequality, and personal satisfaction. Third, we create a sense of belonging: regular gatherings, a Facebook group, and recognition events. The result is that many tutors continue for years, not just the minimum three months. Some have served for over a decade.

中文

招募既容易又困難。容易是因為香港有很多有心人願意幫忙。困難是因為我們要求的是持續的承諾——至少三個月，每週一小時，在固定的時間和地點。這與一次性的義工服務非常不同。我們通過多種渠道招募：大學合作、企業合作、媒體報導、社交媒體和口碑。我們許多導師是曾經受助的學生，現在想回饋社會。留住導師建立在三個支柱上。第一，我們提供培訓和支援：迎新會、每月工作坊、教學資源和同行社群。第二，我們強調互惠：補習對導師的益處不亞於對學生。許多導師表示，這段經歷提供了有意義的連結、對社會不平等的洞察和個人滿足感。第三，我們創造歸屬感：定期聚會、Facebook群組和表彰活動。結果是許多導師持續服務多年，不僅僅是最低的三個月。有些已服務超過十年。

Q9 How do you ensure quality when your tutors are not professional teachers? ▼
Why they ask: To assess governance and quality assurance.
English

We do not require our tutors to be professional teachers. In fact, professional teachers constitute less than three percent of our tutor corps. This is deliberate. Hong Kong teachers already face overwhelming workloads. Their primary responsibility should be to their own students. Instead, we welcome any individual with a secondary school education who possesses patience, empathy, and commitment to educational equity. Quality is ensured through our support infrastructure. First, all tutors attend an orientation session where we explain our philosophy, the one-on-one model, and the principles of "compare only with yourself." Second, we offer monthly training workshops on topics such as teaching strategies, classroom management, and understanding learner diversity. Third, we provide teaching resources, curriculum materials, and an online platform where tutors can share experiences and seek advice. Fourth, regional coordinators facilitate initial tutor-student matching and provide ongoing monitoring and troubleshooting. Fifth, our survey results show that 100% of students affirm that their tutor is helpful and that they would recommend the service to others. This is the strongest evidence of quality. The tutors' greatest asset is not professional qualification but patience, empathy, and genuine care.

中文

我們不要求導師是專業教師。事實上，專業教師在我們的導師團隊中佔不到百分之三。這是刻意的。香港的教師已經面對沉重的工作量。他們的主要責任應該是照顧好自己的學生。相反，我們歡迎任何具有中學教育程度、具備耐心、同理心和對教育平等承諾的人。質素通過我們的支援架構來保證。第一，所有導師都參加迎新會，我們在會上解釋我們的理念、一對一模式和「只與自己比較」的原則。第二，我們每月舉辦培訓工作坊，主題包括教學策略、課堂管理和理解學習多樣性。第三，我們提供教學資源、課程材料和網上平台，讓導師分享經驗和尋求建議。第四，地區統籌員協助最初的師生配對，並提供持續的監察和支援。第五，我們的調查結果顯示，100%的學生確認他們的導師有幫助，並會向他人推薦這項服務。這是質素的最強證據。導師最大的資產不是專業資格，而是耐心、同理心和真誠的關懷。

Q10 What is the "Pay It Forward" effect, and why does it matter? ▼
Why they ask: To assess the sustainability and legacy of your work.
English

18.6% of our scholarship recipients—113 students—have committed to becoming volunteer tutors themselves. This is the most powerful evidence of legacy. One student said, "I have become a tutor myself—passing on what I received." Another said, "It is more blessed to give than to receive." This creates a self-sustaining cycle: students become mentors, and the community grows stronger. This is how we build a legacy that lasts beyond any single individual.

中文

18.6%的獎學金獲得者——113名學生——承諾自己成為義務導師。這是遺產最有力的證據。一位學生說：「我自己也成為了導師——把我收到的傳遞下去。」另一位說：「施比受更為有福。」這創造了一個自我延續的循環：學生成為導師，社群變得更強大。這就是我們如何建立超越任何個人的遺產。

3 Challenges & Growth
Q11 What have been the biggest challenges, and how have you overcome them? ▼
Why they ask: To assess resilience and problem-solving.
English

The biggest challenges have been funding, tutor recruitment, and the scale of need. First, funding: PCFTW receives no government funding. Our operating expenses come entirely from individual donations, foundation grants, and fundraising activities. In the early years, I sent proposal after proposal to foundations with no response. I remember one donor who promised a large sum but attached conditions that would have compromised the purity of our service. I refused. We started again from scratch. Second, tutor recruitment: while we have over 20,000 tutors, we still have over 2,000 students on our waiting list. The demand always exceeds the supply. We address this through continuous recruitment efforts, university partnerships, and media coverage. Third, the scale of need: every day, more students register. We cannot serve them all. But as I have learned from the story of the person picking up starfish on the beach, "it makes a difference to that one." We do what we can, one student at a time. The greatest challenge, however, is not logistical. It is the knowledge that our work is treating symptoms, not the root cause. The real solution lies in systemic reform of mainstream education. Until that happens, we continue to do what we can.

中文

最大的挑戰是資金、導師招募和需求的規模。第一，資金：PCFTW不接受政府資助。我們的營運開支完全來自個人捐款、基金會撥款和籌款活動。早年，我向基金會寄出一份又一份計劃書，卻沒有回音。我記得有一位捐款人承諾一筆大額捐款，但附加條件會損害我們服務的純粹性。我拒絕了。我們從零開始。第二，導師招募：雖然我們有超過20,000名導師，但仍有超過2,000名學生在輪候名單上。需求總是超過供應。我們通過持續的招募工作、大學合作和媒體報導來應對。第三，需求的規模：每天都有更多學生註冊。我們無法服務所有人。但正如我從沙灘上拾海星的人的故事中學到的，「對那一個來說，是有分別的。」我們盡力而為，一次一個學生。然而，最大的挑戰不是後勤上的。而是知道我們的工作是在治標，不是治本。真正的解決方案在於主流教育的系統性改革。在那之前，我們繼續盡力而為。

Q12 What has been your greatest challenge or regret? ▼
Why they ask: To assess humility and self-awareness.
English

My greatest challenge has been accepting my limitations. I have had to learn that I cannot save every student. Some students—despite our best efforts—do not return. Some have family situations too complex to overcome. I remember one student, a hidden youth who had not attended school for two years. I tutored him, befriended him, and after months of patience, he agreed to try returning to school. On the morning he was to leave, he walked to the door, put his hand on the handle, and then stopped. He turned back. He could not do it. I never saw him again. That failure haunts me. I carry the guilt of not being able to help him. But it also taught me something important: we must not judge our success by the number of students we save. We must judge it by the quality of our presence, the sincerity of our effort, and the love we offer. We do what we can. We pick up the starfish we can reach. 8 Personal Reflections
Q32 What has been your greatest joy in this work? ▼
Why they ask: To understand your emotional connection to the work.
English

My greatest joy is witnessing transformation. Not just academic transformation, though that is meaningful—a student who goes from failing to passing, from despair to hope, from being told "you are not good enough" to being admitted to university. But the deeper joy is witnessing the transformation of character. It is seeing a student who once felt ashamed of receiving help become a volunteer tutor themselves. It is seeing a student who was told they would never succeed become a mentor to others. It is seeing the "pay it forward" spirit take root and grow. One of my most cherished memories is of a student who, after receiving tutoring and gaining admission to university, returned to register as a volunteer tutor. She said to me, "I want to pass on the help I received." That, to me, is the greatest reward. It is not about what we give to students. It is about who they become. And when they become givers, when they become mentors, when they become people who believe in others as we believed in them—that is the joy that sustains me.

中文

我最大的喜悅是見證轉變。不僅是學術上的轉變，雖然那也有意義——一個學生從不合格到合格，從絕望到希望，從被說「你不夠好」到被大學錄取。但更深的喜悅是見證品格的轉變。是看到一個曾經為接受幫助而感到羞恥的學生成為義務導師。是看到一個曾被說永遠不會成功的學生成為他人的導師。是看到「傳遞善意」的精神扎根和成長。我最珍惜的回憶之一，是一個學生在接受補習並獲大學錄取後，回來註冊成為義務導師。她對我說：「我想把我收到的幫助傳遞下去。」對我來說，這是最大的回報。不是我們給了學生什麼。而是他們成為了什麼樣的人。當他們成為給予者，當他們成為導師，當他們成為像我們相信他們那樣相信他人的人——這就是支撐我的喜悅。
